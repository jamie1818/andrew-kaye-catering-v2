The secret to hosting a successful event is serving great food.

That is what we have believed since Andrew Kaye Catering was established in 2010, and it is why clients come back to us again and again. Our chefs and waiting staff look after everything on the day, so you can be a guest at your own party.

We cater for all occasions in homes, marquees and venues, and we pride ourselves on delivering the perfect menu with creativity and attention to detail.

In homes, marquees and venues

Weddings

Canapés on arrival, a dinner that suits the room and a late-night salt beef bar if you want one. We work alongside your planner and venue so the day runs smoothly.

Bar and bat mitzvahs

Food stations that get talked about, plates the children will actually eat, and menus that work for guests who keep strictly kosher.

Birthdays and dinner parties at home

Restaurant-standard plates from your own kitchen, or from no kitchen at all. We bring everything we need, including the staff.

Everything else

Anniversaries, engagements, christenings, office lunches and afternoon teas. If people are gathering, talk to us.
